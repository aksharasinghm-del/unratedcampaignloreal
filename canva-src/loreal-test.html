Unrated.
A filter, ads and a public stand that cross out the scores women are given.
1

Show it

Teaser ads show only scores, "6.4", "7/10", so people notice how normal rating women has become.

2

Cross it out

The Unrated AR filter crosses every score out in lipstick red. Women use it to answer their own "rate me" posts.

3

Change it

An open letter asks apps to stop scoring teenagers, signed by the brand and by women everywhere.
